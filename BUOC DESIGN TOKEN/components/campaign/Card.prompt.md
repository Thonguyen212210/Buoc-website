Soft, lifted surface container — the building block for campaign cards, panels, and forms.

```jsx
<Card variant="default" padding="md" hoverable>…</Card>
<Card variant="dark">Light text on the night-sky gradient</Card>
```

Variants: `default` (paper white), `soft` (faint lavender), `dark` (night-sky gradient + light text), `accent` (cream). `hoverable` lifts the card on hover.
